# Harvey's brief for the presentation handover (captured verbatim, 28 Jul)
Not lost — build the full handover document from this on his word.

* Training mode - fully immersive, doesnt mess with stock, good practice for staff to get used to app.
* Pick a site - multi site power (add a new site and boom auto works)
* tap to switch site.
* Live status of app - bring into the closing and opening times of app stuff.
* Scan QR - Opens camera and scans dedicated QR codes for every single part. Helps us track stock correctly as RF isnt accurate (parts dont get put in, messes with stock levels). With a torch for poor WS lighting if required in dark spots. Can also type the SKU if needed.
* Search - find any part, stock level, when it will run out, where it physically is with our new shelving system (rows and shelves), SKU matches, name matches RF (in future) & price listed. Also says predicted when the stock will run out and has a colour designated to that. Take a part and it instantly updates.
* Low Stock - Warning system for stock and whats going to run out when and stock levels. Describe how the smart system works for ordering levels, the more the app runs the smarter it becomes. Use 50mm silent blocks as example, we use lets say 150 in 30 days it predicts we need another 150 for the next 30 days, then learns again.
* Request Order - Request parts, add additonal stuff like soap and gloves. it sends notifcation to Ross and Harvey. and stores the notification in the MANAGER DASH until sorted.
* Manager Dashboard - See active users on app today, how stock looks overview, basic overview of everything, todays repairs, Racefacer Stock discrepancies. Notifications page needs updating and the whole page needs cleaning up at a later date. To do list shows jobs that need doing in the app.
* Finance - Lots of charts showing financial data. Plenty of other data, again needs refining and cleaning up.
* Generate Order - extremely helpful, discuss how it generates the order and what formulas, breaks down by site, can change when you wanna order date. Download order as PDF or CSV. Show example.
* TV Display, running board in WS very useful for quick understanding of how we looking, can send emergencies, messages & a friendly leaderboard comp of whos got most repairs.
* App Health - new addition, needs work. Looks at how the app is functioning, the database, our render workers, predicted monthly costs, and other stuff.
* Daily Summary - a detailed overview of the day, going back 14 days. See how well mechanics have performed and whats been fixed.
* Master Access - THE BRAIN! The app completly editable from inside the app.
* Parts - Displays each part - SKU, supplier, full name, internal name, price in EUR which constantly updates to AUD, min stock levels we set some items (others calc themselves based on useage talk about this formula), yield rate, location, Can bin and add parts. See QR code. Download as PDF or 3mf or STL file individually. QR generated on SKU so never fails.
* Staff - Add and delete staff. Give them 4 digit pin entry (FACE ID coming), set them a role and set their access to sites.
* Sites - add and remove sites (good for if we expand further)
* QR Editor - Completely design printable QR codes in the app and make any adjustments
* Tracks - Ignore for now - will likely change this. Ross can handle this.
* Karts - Long term wifi and new fleet issues or karts without the correct parts defect a true kart count and the max potential the workshop can produce. This helps put karts out of the kart counts and making it simpler, "what can actually be fixed?".
* Ramp Access - we require to clear our ramp to give access to level 6 during busy periods, sends to phones and TV screen!
* Access - Control which roles and individuals can see and do in the app.
* Phones - See whos connected
* Settings - change opening times for app (saves database usage) and auto sign out.
* Account - change profile pic and put notificiations on
* Comms
* Tasks - set tasks, talk about and add comments.
* Chats - Group chats and individual - all send push notifications
* Handovers - Will eventually link to Front so people can send from phones and read clearly with ease
* HK AI - Ask lots (help explain this)
* Kart Info
* Karts - Full RF screen. See status of kart by colour. Kart colours match RF. Red and green dots linked to RIMO instant showing if karts online. Health bar shows ratio of OK, FM and Damaged. Shows useable rate also. Change kart status, make repairs and notes, change admin so swapping karts between tracks becomes super easy, history of karts.
* Notes - Same as RF.
* Repairs - Same as RF
* Wiring - Eventually be a 3D model of wiring diagram you can click wiring lines and see clearly where power goes in the kart - very helpful diagnosing electrical issues

Target: Claude Design software. Every single feature, maximum detail.
